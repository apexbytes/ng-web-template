Security Solutions

Elevated Intelligence for
Proactive Asset Protection

GeoMapping Pty Ltd delivers advanced aerial security solutions designed for large industrial sites, remote assets, and critical infrastructure. Our service leverages drone-based aerial intelligence integrated with ground-based personnel to provide real-time insight and early threat identification.

By adding an elevated layer of oversight, we help security teams reduce blind spots, improve response times, and maintain operational confidence in high-risk environments across South Africa.

  • Real-time aerial monitoring and early threat detection
  • Seamless integration with existing ground-based security teams
  • Intelligent protection for mining, industrial, and public assets
