Mining Surveying

Mining Decisions Backed
by Precise, Reliable Data

Mining decisions are only as good as the data behind them. At GeoMapping Pty Ltd, our mining surveying and planning solutions are built to give you confidence at every stage of your operation — from early planning through active production and ongoing compliance.

We specialise in mine-focused geospatial and survey services that support safe, efficient, and well-controlled mining environments. Whether you are operating an open cast or underground mine, accurate spatial data is critical — and that is exactly what we deliver.

  • SACAA-certified team with deep mining sector experience
  • Solutions tailored for real operating conditions across South Africa
  • Supporting statutory, compliance, and reporting requirements

What This Service Covers

01

Open Cast & Underground Mine Surveys

Comprehensive surface and underground spatial data collection using the latest survey equipment, providing accurate records for mine control and planning.

02

Mine Layout & Planning Support

Precise spatial data and GIS-driven mine layout services that support operational planning, design, and long-term development decision-making.

03

Production & Volume Measurement Surveys

Regular production surveys and volumetric calculations that give mine operators real-time visibility into extraction rates, material movement, and progress.

04

Stockpile & Material Movement Verification

Accurate stockpile volume measurements and material movement tracking using drone photogrammetry and conventional survey methods for reliable reconciliation.

05

Statutory, Compliance & Reporting Surveys

All statutory mine survey requirements met accurately and on schedule — supporting legal compliance, audit readiness, and regulatory reporting obligations.
