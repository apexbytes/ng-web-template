Our Story

Leading the Way in
Geospatial Intelligence

GeoMapping Pty Ltd is the top provider of advanced Mining Surveying, Planning, and Geospatial solutions in South Africa. Leveraging over five years of industry experience and a highly qualified, SACAA-certified team, we are committed to delivering precision and reliability across all our operations.

Our journey began with a vision to transform the mining and construction sectors through the intelligent application of drone technology and advanced sensors. Today, we serve clients nationwide, providing the critical data needed for safe and efficient operations.
