Aerial Survey & GIS

High-Precision Geospatial

GeoMapping Pty Ltd delivers advanced aerial survey and GIS services using state-of-the-art drone technology, LiDAR sensors, and spatial analysis tools. We provide our clients with accurate, actionable geospatial intelligence that traditional ground surveys simply cannot match in speed or scale.

What This Service Covers

01

High-Resolution Photogrammetry

Capturing thousands of high-resolution images to create accurate orthomosaics, 3D point clouds, and textured mesh models of any site.

02

LiDAR Scanning & Terrain Modeling

Using laser-based scanning to penetrate vegetation and capture precise ground elevations, creating highly accurate Digital Terrain Models (DTM).

03

GIS Spatial Analysis & Mapping

Turning raw data into intelligence through spatial modeling, database management, and customized map production for planning and oversight.

04

Thermal & Multi-Spectral Imaging

Specialized sensors for identifying heat signatures, water leaks, or vegetation health — critical for environmental and industrial monitoring.

05

Orthophotography & Topographic Maps

Producing distortion-free, scale-accurate aerial photographs and detailed contour maps used as base layers for engineering design.
