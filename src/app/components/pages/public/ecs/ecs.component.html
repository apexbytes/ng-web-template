Engineering Survey

Precision Layouts for
Modern Infrastructure

GeoMapping Pty Ltd provides professional engineering and construction survey services that support accurate project delivery from initial planning through to final completion. Our team works closely with engineers, architects, and project managers to ensure every element is positioned exactly where it belongs.

Using advanced total stations, GPS/GNSS, and 3D laser scanners, we deliver the high-precision data required for complex infrastructure, commercial builds, and industrial developments across South Africa.

  • Setting out and construction layout for civil and structural projects
  • Detailed as-built surveys for design verification and compliance
  • Continuous deformation and structural stability monitoring

What This Service Covers

01

Setting Out Surveys

Transferring design coordinates from the drawing board to the site with extreme precision, marking foundations, grids, and utilities.

02

As-Built Verification

Providing final records of the finished construction to verify that the build matches the design and meets all regulatory standards.

03

Deformation Monitoring

Tracking movement in buildings, bridges, and terrain over time to ensure structural integrity and early identification of potential risks.

04

Topographic Site Mapping

Creating highly detailed base maps of existing terrain and features to support architectural design and civil engineering planning.

05

Infrastructure Monitoring

Regular safety and stability checks on critical infrastructure such as roads, dams, and bridges using high-precision survey methods.
